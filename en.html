Camino Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026 · Versión en español · Términos de uso

Camino is a calisthenics training app. This policy explains what data it handles and what it does with it.

Camino collects no data. There are no accounts, no server, no analytics and no advertising. The app sends none of your data anywhere: everything you record is stored only on your device. The only connection in the whole app is the YouTube tutorial player, and only if you tap it — explained below.

What data the app handles, and where it stays

Camino stores the following in a local database inside your phone:

  • Your training profile: days per week, minutes per session, available equipment, experience level and goal.
  • Your progress: completed sessions, logged sets and repetitions, personal records and progression steps reached.
  • Joint discomfort and mobility limitations that you declare.
  • Body measurements you enter: weight, height and the results of the posture test.
  • Your preferences: colour theme, sound, vibration and reminders.

None of this leaves the device. It is not synchronised, it is not backed up to any cloud, and the developer has no way of seeing it.

Permissions the app requests

  • Notifications: to tell you when a rest period ends, and for training reminders if you enable them. Notifications are generated on the device; none are received from any server.
  • Vibration: for the timer alert.
  • Background audio playback: so the timer alert sounds with the screen off.
  • Internet: only for the YouTube tutorial player, and only when you tap it. The app does not use it for anything else.

The app does not request access to the camera, the microphone, contacts, location or your personal files.

The YouTube tutorials

Exercise pages have a button to watch a tutorial. The player does not load on its own: it appears only when you tap it. If you never tap one, the app makes no network connection at all.

When you do tap it, YouTube's official player opens inside the app, and that is a connection to Google. As with any page that embeds a video, YouTube receives your IP address and may use cookies, and whatever it does with that is governed by Google's privacy policy, not this one.

Camino sends it nothing of yours: not your profile, not your progress, not your measurements, not an identifier. The only thing that travels is which video you asked for.

Backups

Camino lets you export your progress to a file. You generate that file, it stays wherever you save it, and you share it with whoever you choose. The app does not upload it anywhere.

Minors

Camino is for people aged 18 and over and does not advise minors. The app is not directed at them and does not adapt training to a growing body. See the terms of use (Spanish) for detail.

In any case the app collects no data from anyone, regardless of age, because it collects no data at all.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes the local database and with it everything Camino knows about you. Nothing remains anywhere else, because nothing was ever anywhere else. If you want to keep your progress before uninstalling, export a backup from Settings.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app were to start collecting data, this policy would be updated before that version shipped and the change would be announced inside the app.
